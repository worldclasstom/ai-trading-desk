# AI Trading Desk

Your AI is the analyst. [Tally](https://tally.markets) is the risk officer.
This template helps you write a thesis before entering, review evidence against
its exit rules, and keep decisions accountable across conversations.

## Set up

Ask your AI:

> Read https://tally.markets/desk.md and help me set up my Tally journal.

Or follow [Connect your AI](https://tally.markets/connect), sign in, and ask:
“Get started with Tally, then run my checkup.” Tool availability depends on your
AI app, plan and permissions. No broker connection is required.

Keep [the protocol](AGENTS.md) in your AI project's instructions if supported.
The hosted `get_started` response is the current protocol.

| Prompt | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Morning checkup](prompts/morning-checkup.md) | Review evidence and deadlines |
| [Idea to thesis](prompts/idea-to-thesis.md) | Draft an idea before saving |
| [Entry review](prompts/execution-day.md) | Review readiness; no order staging |
| [Close-out](prompts/close-out.md) | Record a close and review the process |

Free includes 3 active theses (drafts included) and 10 total created. Sentinel
is $4.99/month or $49/year. Saved history stays readable, reviewable and closable.
Cooling-off is optional. Theses and closed records stay private unless you
explicitly publish a receipt. Scheduling depends on the client; monitoring
covers supported sources and cannot guarantee every criterion is checked.

Tally MCP tools modify journal records, never brokerage orders. The human makes
the trading decision and handles actual execution separately. Existing optional
Propr controls are separate in Tally Settings. Read [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md).
