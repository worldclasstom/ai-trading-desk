# Close-out

Run when a criterion has fired, the window has closed, or you want out.

```
Walk me through closing my thesis "[title]" in Tally. If a criterion
fired, the exit reason is that criterion — hold me to it. If I'm exiting
on discretion, make me write the justification in my own words before
you record it; push back once if my reason is really just discomfort.
Then close it, and give me the receipt link.
```

Every close mints a public receipt graded separately on process and
outcome. A rule-following loss grades better than a lucky override —
that is the point of the desk.
