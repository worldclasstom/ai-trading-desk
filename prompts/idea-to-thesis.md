# Idea → thesis

Run when a trade idea is worth taking seriously. The assistant's job is
to sharpen it into something falsifiable before Tally records it.

```
I have a trade idea: [describe it].

Pressure-test it with me, then pre-register it in Tally: a falsifiable
thesis statement, the invalidation criteria that would prove it wrong,
the dissemination criteria that would mean the edge is gone, and a
catalyst window. Tell me anything that resists being written down that
precisely — that's a hole in the idea, not in the form.
```

Choose whether to use the optional 24-hour cooling-off before saving. A complete
thesis without cooling-off is immediately ready as a journal record, not an order.
